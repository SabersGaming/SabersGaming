Greetings

Greetings, adventuring incrementalists. I've gone by the nickname of Saber for quite some time. My name is Eric, and welcome to my corner of the internet. I first started gaming way back when the Atari 2600 came out and I've held every major console since. Back when the Commodore 64 was out, my love for text based adventure games was born. Zork series, pretty much everything infocom and the original Phantasie are among my favorites. Also, at that time, i used to play and occasionally run AD&D games, Robotech, Cyberpunk, and many others. In recent years, I've discovered incremental games and they are very addicting for me. Now I have the opportunity to combine my love for 2 things and share them with you. I've always dreamt of the day that i could let my visions become stories and now here we are. Join me on this journey through my visions. I hope you enjoy your stay.

Origins - Malakars Descent

Blurb

Before the darkness returned, it had to fall.

Malakar was once a man. A scholar who sought forbidden knowledge in ancient tomes of necromancy.

One corpse. Ten. Hundreds. Thousands.

With each resurrection, his humanity slips away. His tower of black stone becomes a monument to death itself. And the people of Brooklynia realize too late that the man they once knew is gone—replaced by something far more terrifying.

Every villain has an origin.

Brooklynia - Darkness Returns

Blurb

For five centuries, Brooklynia has been shrouded in darkness.

Arintha Cradleborn is a warrior who commands the elements—and asks few questions. When the town elder sends her to hunt bandits on a simple tracking job, she expects nothing more than another payday.

She's wrong.

Betrayed and captured, Arintha wakes in a dungeon beneath the earth, caught in an ancient conspiracy she doesn't understand. A shattered sword. A necromancer's dark ritual. Fifteen fragments scattered across the land.

And a prophecy that marks her as the only one who can stop what's coming.

But in a world where magic corrupts and allies become enemies, survival is just the beginning.

The darkness has returned. And it's hungry.

CM - CorpseMatter

The tower. This is probably going to aggravate a lot of you. I wanted to add an element that didn't tell you what you needed and you needed to learn by trial and error. I'm going to give you a hint, zombies/gatherers, ghoul/foreman, golem/builder. And the cost will start off a little of everything and kind of low at first and scale gradually, have fun! There's features that will unlock as you progress.

3 curses(spells) they're still in development but they work. Terror Raids unlock at level 10. Once you do a few grimoire resets it'll get gradually easier. There are boss fights that i have yet to test out.
