Welcome to Sabers Gaming.

I've been building worlds in my head for as long as I can remember. What you'll find here is the result of that — stories, games, and universes that started as a vision and are finally finding their way into the world.

Right now, two major works are in motion. Malakar's Descent — a game based on Origins of Brooklynia: Malakar's Descent, the origin story that predates the main series — is nearing completion for Android and is currently available for public beta testing. On the literary side, I'm putting the final touches on the first trilogy of Tales of Brooklynia, a planned ten-book epic fantasy series that builds toward a final confrontation in a standalone tenth book.

Current Projects

  • Malakar's Descent (Android) — Public Beta Now Available
  • Tales of Brooklynia Trilogy One — Final Touches Underway
  • Origins of Brooklynia: Malakar's Descent — The origin story behind the game

Everything will be made available here as it's ready. This is only the beginning.

About Sabers Gaming

Welcome to Sabers Gaming, home of the Brooklynia Chronicles and beyond. I'm the creator behind this world of incremental storytelling — a lifelong gamer, author, and world-builder.

My gaming roots go all the way back to the Atari 2600, through the golden age of text adventures like Zork and the Infocom classics, tabletop RPGs like AD&D, Robotech, and Cyberpunk, and everything in between. That love of deep narrative and interactive worlds is the foundation of everything I create here.

Sabers Gaming is where storytelling meets game mechanics — incremental adventures set in richly developed worlds, starting with the epic fantasy universe of Brooklynia.
