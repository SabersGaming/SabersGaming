📜 THE WORLD OF BROOKLYNIA

Before Malakar, there was a boy. Before the boy became a monster, he was simply right — and punished for it.

Brooklynia is a land of ten factions and fifteen sovereign cities, held together by a treaty called the Covenant of Seven Cities — a fragile truce that everyone observes in public and violates in private. Three factions now stand against you:

THE IRONCLAD REPUBLIC — A merchant empire governed by the Board of Iron, whose every decision is made by vote and every vote can be bought.
THE CHURCH OF THE RADIANT SUN — Devotees of Sol-Auris, the sun god, who consider undeath the deepest possible desecration of light.
THE COURT OF MIDNIGHT — A criminal empire structured like a government, deeply invested in your failure.
Malakar was gifted — genuinely, undeniably gifted — and the other apprentice mages knew it. So they mocked him. He descended into the old crypts where the ley lines run black and cold, and he learned what the academies refused to teach.
