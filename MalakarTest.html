The Necromancer's Codex — Game Guide
Quick Start Guide ▲
  1. Raise Zombies (10 SE each) to generate Soul Essence per tick.
  2. Click 'Study' (50 DM) to boost Dark Mana generation.
  3. Purchase Tomes of Binding to increase zombie production rate and unit caps.
  4. Accumulate 1,000,000 SE then click 'Ascend' to reach Level 2 and multiply your power.
  5. At Level 2, Ghouls and Golems unlock better production. At Level 5, Corpse Matter use begins.
  6. At Level 20, the Grimoire unlocks — choose your first permanent power before resetting.
  7. Complete Quests to earn Tome Upgrade Points for the Dark Library.
Resources ▼

Soul Essence (SE)

Your primary currency. Generated each tick by your undead army. Used to buy units and perform Dark Ascension (cost: 1,000,000 × 2^(level-1)).

Dark Mana (DM)

Magical energy generated by Tomes of Sight, army probability, and the Study action. Used for Ghouls, Golems, Tomes, Grimoire skills, and Curses.

Corpse Matter (CM)

Rare physical essence. Generated by random 2% chance per tick, certain skills, and Tower floors. Used for Grimoire skills and Tower construction.

Tome Upgrade Points (TUP)

Earned by completing Quests. Spent in the Dark Library to increase Tome caps beyond 75. Persists through all resets.

Units ▼

Zombie Thralls (10 SE)

Your basic undead. Cap: 25×Level^1.3 + Binding×15. Production scales with Tomes of Binding.

Ghouls (100 SE + 10 DM)

Faster undead with Dark Mana cost. Cap: 12×Level^1.3 + Binding×8. Produce 5 SE base per tick.

Golems (1000 SE + 50 DM)

Powerful constructs. Cap: 6×Level^1.3 + Sight×4. Produce 20 SE base per tick.

Wraiths (5000 SE + 100 DM) — Grimoire Unlock

Spectral elite units. Unlocked by 'Army of Darkness' skill. Cap: 5×Level^1.2. Produce 50 SE + CM each tick.

Tomes & Dark Library ▼

Tome of Binding (100 DM)

Increases zombie production rate and raises Zombie/Ghoul caps. Cap starts at 75, upgradeable via Dark Library (+1 cap per TUP).

Tome of Sight (250 DM)

Directly adds DM per tick and raises Golem caps. Cap starts at 75, upgradeable via Dark Library.

Dark Library

Unlocked when BOTH tome types are at their current max caps. Spend TUP to increase caps by +1 each. Tomes and caps persist through ALL resets.

Dark Ascension ▼

Ascend when you have enough SE (1M × 2^(level-1)). Resets SE, DM, CM, and units. Increases your permanent multiplier equal to your new level.

PERSISTS: Tower floors, Tomes, Grimoire skills, raid/boss progress.

SPECIAL — Level 20: First time reaching level 20, only SE is deducted. DM, CM, and units remain so you can immediately choose Grimoire skills.

Curses ▼

Feast of Souls (Level 2, 100 DM)

Doubles SE production for 60 seconds. No cooldown.

Corpse Gratification (Level 5, 10k SE + 750 DM)

Instantly gain 10 Corpse Matter. 5 minute cooldown.

Ruinous Ambition (Level 10, 250 DM)

+100% SE production, -50% DM generation for 5 minutes. 10 minute cooldown.

The Grimoire ▼

Unlocks at Level 20. Click Grimoire to PAUSE the game and enter skill selection. Purchase permanent skills with your current DM and CM, then click CONFIRM PRESTIGE to reset and keep those skills forever.

23 skills across 5 tiers. Each tier requires 4 skills from the previous tier. Tier 5 culminates in 'The Final Ascension' — which unlocks the final boss.

The Tower ▼

Build 5 floors of your Black Tower. Each requires resources and an army to construct. Each floor has a failure chance — if it fails, resources are lost and you must try again. Floors survive Dark Ascension but reset on Grimoire Prestige.

Terror Raids ▼

Unlocks at Level 10. Choose targets (Village, Monastery, Graveyard, Castle) and strategies (Cautious/Standard/Reckless). 20% chance for special narrative raids. Retaliation can cost you units.

Last 25%: When 75% of raid time passes, dramatic live updates begin appearing as the outcome unfolds.

Boss Battles ▼

Unlocks after mastering any Tier 2 Grimoire skill. Face 15 champions of Brooklynia and then the Final Boss: Hope, the Eternal Flame. Each Grimoire Prestige unlocks one new boss. Use your combat skills to deal damage before the boss kills your army.

Factions of Brooklynia ▼

Ironclad Republic

Mercantile military faction — generals, merchants, politicians. Five of their champions stand between you and victory.

Church of the Radiant Sun

Holy warriors, priests, and templars wielding sacred light against your darkness. Five champions.

Court of Midnight

Assassins, spies, and shadow brokers who rule the underworld. Five champions — and ironically your most dangerous adversaries.

📜 THE WORLD OF BROOKLYNIA

Before Malakar, there was a boy. Before the boy became a monster, he was simply right — and punished for it.

Brooklynia is a land of ten factions and fifteen sovereign cities, held together by a treaty called the Covenant of Seven Cities — a fragile truce that everyone observes in public and violates in private. Three factions now stand against you:

THE IRONCLAD REPUBLIC — A merchant empire governed by the Board of Iron, whose every decision is made by vote and every vote can be bought. They distrust magic and replace it with steam-driven machines wherever possible.
THE CHURCH OF THE RADIANT SUN — Devotees of Sol-Auris, the sun god, who believe every soul carries a divine inner spark. They consider undeath the deepest possible desecration of that light.
THE COURT OF MIDNIGHT — A criminal empire structured like a government, operating from the fog-shrouded city of Shadowhaven. Sophisticated. Principled, in their own way. And deeply invested in your failure.

Magic in Brooklynia flows through invisible currents called ley lines. The darkest tradition — necromancy — draws on the vital force released at the moment of death, channeling it through the practitioner's will. The academies called it profane. They called him worse.

Malakar was gifted — genuinely, undeniably gifted — and the other apprentice mages knew it. So they mocked him. Not for weakness, but for the particular cruelty reserved for those who cannot be beaten fairly. They drove him from the halls of the Arcane Consortium with laughter and whispered slander, not blades. He left without a word. He descended into the deep places of the world, into the old crypts where the ley lines run black and cold, and he learned what the academies refused to teach. He did not choose necromancy because it was dark. He chose it because it was the one discipline they told him he must never touch — and he had decided, in that long cold silence beneath the earth, that he would never again be told what he must not have.

The armies he raises, the factions he dismantles, the empire he builds from bone and shadow — these are not merely conquest. They are a reply. Long overdue. Delivered at considerable volume.
